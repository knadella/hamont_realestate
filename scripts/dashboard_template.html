Where each metric stands — all property types

Supply vs. demand

Two counts on one scale: homes actively listed (supply, orange) and homes sold per month (demand, blue). Through 2021 the lines nearly touched — almost everything listed sold. As supply pulls above demand, the widening gap between the lines is the market softening toward buyers.

Active listings (supply) Homes sold (demand)

Market balance — months of inventory

Active listings ÷ homes sold = how many months to clear all inventory at the current pace. It is the cleanest single read on who holds the leverage: under ~2 months is a seller's market, ~2–4 balanced, above ~4 a buyer's market. Hamilton has crossed the whole spectrum since 2016.

Year-over-year, by year (all property types)

By property type — detached vs. semi vs. condo

The "all types" number hides a wide split. Detached homes are usually the tightest segment; condo apartments tend to carry the most inventory and the softest prices; semi-detached sits between but trades in low volumes, so its month-to-month figures are noisy.
